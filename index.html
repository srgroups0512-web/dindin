Reassurance, not surveillance

A clear safety rhythm, without constant tracking.

Din Din asks for Always location access during onboarding for safety escalation. It shares one fresh or last-known location when an alert is triggered; it does not continuously track you, and your circle does not need the app.

Privacy by design

Trust is part of the product.

Din Din stores the account, circle, reminder, response, subscription, and most recently captured safety location needed to provide the service. Location is shared only during a missed or user-triggered safety alert.
