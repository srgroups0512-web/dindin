What will be deleted

  • Your Din Din account and profile
  • Circle and primary-contact records
  • Reminder schedules and response history
  • Saved safety location and push tokens

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute purposes. Residual copies may remain in protected backups until their normal deletion cycle completes.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your Din Din account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it through the applicable store.
